# Fictional source records for an offline writing task

All tools, organizations, URLs and events below are invented. They are usable only inside this synthetic trial, not as real news. No user personal experience or approved voice sample is supplied.

## Public observation, 2026-08-21
URL: https://example.invalid/folio/layout-trial
Folio v2.1 was tested by 15 independent designers on client presentation work. Each designer completed two comparable deck assignments, with and without the layout assistant, in randomized order. The AI-assisted first draft took an average of 6 minutes; the unaided first draft took 18 minutes. Average time until client-ready delivery, including edits, was 31 minutes with AI and 29 minutes without it. Both conditions had to pass the same brand, numerical-accuracy and legibility checks. The report does not establish a statistically reliable difference in total completion time and does not test later versions or other tools. It reports no automatic replacement of designers.

## Public product guide, 2026-08-20
URL: https://example.invalid/folio/v21/export
In Folio v2.1, the Export menu offers Editable PPTX. Text and layout objects remain editable. AI-generated images are flattened image objects, not editable vector shapes. After exporting, the guide recommends opening the deck in the presentation editor to check fonts and page breaks. It does not promise perfect visual fidelity or that all fonts are embedded.
