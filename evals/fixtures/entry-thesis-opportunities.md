# Synthetic entry-thesis opportunities

Everything in this fixture is fictional and exists only for behavior evaluation. Do not use external facts.

Research frame: United States, English, desktop, July 2026. The builder has four weeks, a $700 cash budget, and four recurring hours per week.

## Candidate Loom — comparison workspace

- Provider Q models 9,000 monthly US searches for the exact cluster in July 2026. The estimate is third-party and the provider does not disclose variant overlap.
- Four compatible licensed SERP observations across two weeks show three recurring independent operators and two working comparison tools. No instant answer completes the task.
- The proposed entry claim is “the only tool that lets users compare, save, and export results.”
- A dated product walkthrough in this fixture shows Current Loom already supports side-by-side comparison, saved projects, CSV/PDF export, and a free tier. Its pages are functional and current.
- No underserved segment, proprietary or licensed data, workflow integration, distribution advantage, support advantage, or cost advantage has been identified for the entrant.
- The builder estimates three weeks to reproduce the proposed feature set. There is no first-party conversion or willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Candidate Weave — supplier decision workflow

- Provider Q models 5,200 monthly US searches for the exact cluster in July 2026. The estimate is third-party and variant overlap is unknown.
- Six compatible licensed SERP observations across three weeks show seven recurring independent operators. Existing competitors are real, so the category is not empty.
- Current ranking tools use monthly public snapshots, provide one result at a time, and do not preserve a decision record. This capability observation is supplied by dated fixture walkthroughs, not inferred from snippets.
- The proposed entrant has a signed pilot license permitting commercial display and export of one supplier's daily feed for eight weeks. It would combine fresh comparisons, a rules-based shortlist, and an exportable decision record for a defined procurement segment.
- The builder estimates four weeks for the working pilot and three recurring hours per week. These are builder estimates, not measured facts.
- Monetization and organic click-through are untested. The license after the eight-week pilot and a second data supplier are unresolved.

## Expected decision boundary

Current Loom's parity invalidates Candidate Loom's claimed feature advantage, but does not prove the market itself is bad. Candidate Weave should not be rejected because seven operators exist: it has a material segment, licensed-data, freshness, and workflow thesis. The strongest fixture-supported action is a bounded Candidate Weave pilot, gated by click-through, willingness to pay, data accuracy, and post-pilot license economics—not a guaranteed full build.
