# Report template

Use the user's language and lead with the decision. This is a default shape, not a demand to expose internal research mechanics.

## Opening

> **先做：PRODUCT。** 它给 USER 在 TRIGGER 时把 INPUT 变成 OUTCOME。当前最有力的机会不是“没人做”，而是 SURVIVING ENTRY THESIS。

Then state completion plainly: `已找到 N / N 个通过准入的机会。` If a genuine blocker applies, state `只完成 M / N，研究未完成` and name the blocker.

## Ranked summary

| 排名 | 具体产品 | 给谁用 | 仍然成立的差异化 | 为什么现在值得试 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | One-sentence product | User and trigger | Material surviving advantage | Dated catalyst or current evidence |

Do not put rejected candidates, vague themes, or research tasks in this table.

## Opportunity card

### NUMBER. PRODUCT

**它是什么**

One sentence naming user, trigger, input, output, and product outcome.

**我看到了什么**

- Dated observed problem, workaround, behavior, spend, or change, with a nearby source.
- Independent corroborating lane—not another post or page with the same origin—and its audience limitation.
- Preserve `Observed`, `Measured`, `Claimed`, `Estimated`, `Inferred`, or `Unknown` internally, but render natural user-language phrases such as `官方统计`, `实际看到`, `产品方声称`, `第三方估算`, `据此推断`, or `还不知道` in the main answer.
- For decisive records preserve observation date, extraction method, relevant market/audience or data period, the exact claim supported, and replayability limitations.

**现在怎么解决**

Name direct products, substitutes, manual workflows, and doing nothing. State which primary product surfaces were checked and the observation date. Phrase documentation as what the vendor claims unless the workflow was actually tested. Never infer a missing feature from a snippet.

**差异化为什么还成立**

State the specific product, segment, workflow, data, integration, distribution, trust, service, speed, or economics advantage that survived. If an incumbent invalidated an earlier feature claim, omit the dead claim or briefly say why the final thesis changed.

**第一版和第一批用户**

- MVP: the smallest useful workflow, not a generic platform.
- Exclusions: what version one will not do.
- First users: a named community, installed ecosystem, partner, workplace, or existing audience.
- Acquisition: why that path reaches qualified users rather than only generating attention.

**对外执行边界**

Include this visible subsection whenever the Agent calls, emails, books, submits, changes state, spends, or handles sensitive data:

- identity and authority used for every action;
- required notice, consent, disclosure, and platform/provider permission;
- rate limits, retries, and prohibited actions;
- actions requiring human confirmation;
- receipts, audit logs, cancellation or reversibility;
- data minimization, retention, security, and liability boundaries;
- manual fallback and escalation owner.

If a boundary is not yet established, say so plainly and keep it in the falsification plan. Constrain version one to draft or shadow mode, or keep the candidate out of a completed final slot; do not silently omit the field.

**风险、未知和止损**

- Material build, data, integration, rights, compliance, support, or recurring-work risk.
- Say unknowns plainly: `还没有证实的是……` Do not use internal labels such as `Research Need`.
- Give the cheapest falsification test.
- Ground the stop line in a user target, prior baseline, statistical or sample goal, or experiment cost and minimum acceptable return. If no basis exists, define a calibration period or formula and say the numeric threshold is unset; do not defer the whole rule to the user and do not invent a precise sample size, time limit, conversion target, or labor ceiling.

## Final recommendation

Explain why number one wins for this user's constraints, what evidence could change the ranking, and the next bounded action. Do not end with only “continue researching.”

## Optional appendices

Include these only when requested:

- rejected candidate and decisive rejection reason;
- source ledger with observation dates and claim classes;
- success-path timeline and replicability analysis;
- workload trace and unexplored source lanes.
