# Solo business decision rubric

Use this rubric to compare finalists, not to manufacture certainty. Apply hard gates first and leave unsupported dimensions `unknown`.

## Hard gates

Reject or redesign an idea that requires:

- Deceptive or nonfunctional pages.
- Unauthorized automated access, scraping, or personal-data use.
- Material without a workable license.
- Regulated, safety-critical, or high-liability advice beyond the operator's capacity.
- An API, dataset, inventory, or capital commitment outside the stated budget.
- Scaled pages whose primary purpose is ranking rather than independent user value.

Treat unresolved core data rights, licensing, privacy, or regulatory status as a decisive unknown that blocks `Build now`, even when the initial implementation appears cheap.

## Comparative dimensions

Rate each as `strong`, `mixed`, `weak`, or `unknown` and cite the decisive evidence.

| Dimension | Strong evidence | Weak evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| User job | Repeated, costly or valuable task; product completes it | Vague interest; content already completes the task |
| Demand | Current market-specific evidence across compatible sources | Social excitement or stale/unscoped volume only |
| SERP opening | Page-level gap the proposed product can materially fill | Low domain metric or low KD without page audit |
| Click necessity | Interaction, fresh data, workflow, transaction, or primary source is needed | AI/snippet/official result completes the task |
| Founder advantage | Existing code, expertise, data, audience, or distribution | No relevant edge and high learning burden |
| MVP | Credible product in the available time and cash | Demo is easy but trustworthy product is not |
| Operations | Low, bounded, automatable, and affordable | Continuous curation, support, moderation, or sales |
| Monetization | Identified payer plus observed comparable behavior or a first-party test | Revenue inferred from traffic, generic CPC, or an unsupported RPM/conversion assumption |
| Defensibility | Compounding data, workflow, brand, links, or community | Replicable pages and commodity generation |
| Risk/reversibility | Low liability and reusable assets if it fails | Lock-in, legal exposure, sunk cost, or platform dependence |

## Decision rule

- `Build now` requires no hard gate, no decisive unknown, and a credible MVP plus acquisition test. Its cost model must cover development, acquisition, content/data, compliance, infrastructure, and recurring operations where applicable.
- `Bounded test` is appropriate when one or two decisive uncertainties have cheap falsification tests.
- `Watchlist` requires a named external trigger or future dataset; otherwise it is just an unprioritized idea.
- `Do not build` names the structural blocker and avoids disguising rejection as an endless validation plan.

If two ideas are close, prefer the one with faster evidence, lower recurring operations, more reusable assets, and a clearer stop line—not the one with the largest modeled traffic number.
